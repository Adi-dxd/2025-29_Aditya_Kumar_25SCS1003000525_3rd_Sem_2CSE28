## 📌 About the Internship

The internship at **InAmigos Foundation** was a two-week practical experience focused on using creative communication and graphic design to support social-impact initiatives.

During the internship, I worked on awareness-oriented creative content and communication related to the foundation's activities, including **Project SEWA – Food & Clothing Distribution**.

The experience also provided an opportunity to understand the importance of **fundraising, teamwork, communication, coordination, and consistent effort** in supporting social initiatives.

One of the most important learnings for me was understanding that design is not only about creating visually attractive content. A good design can also communicate a social message clearly, attract attention, and encourage people to participate in a cause.
